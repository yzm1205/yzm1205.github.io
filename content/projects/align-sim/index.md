**Auburn University**

- Released software and data for task-free evaluation of sentence embeddings using five semantic
  similarity alignment criteria.
- Evaluated 13 classical and LLM-induced encoders and demonstrated that strong downstream benchmark
  performance can coexist with failures on intuitive semantic alignment tests.
